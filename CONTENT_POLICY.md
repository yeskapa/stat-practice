# Content policy

The MIT licence covers this project's code, documentation and four original demo questions. It does not grant rights to exam papers, passages, diagrams, artwork or answer keys obtained elsewhere.

## Local practice

Use the [source catalogue](MOCK_SOURCES.md) and [import skill](skills/import-mock/SKILL.md) to prepare a paper locally. Downloaded sources, converted papers and attempt data are ignored by Git and excluded from exports. Importing a paper does not grant permission to redistribute it.

## Contributing question packs

Public packs must contain original material or have documented permission covering every included question, passage and image. Public availability and attribution alone do not establish redistribution rights.

Record source URLs, rightsholders, licence or permission terms, distribution scope and required attribution. Independently review complete stimuli, options, answers and explanations before proposing a pack. Preserve source numbering and option order, and explain adapted timing. Add approved files explicitly to the export allowlist; never copy a source collection recursively.

Do not submit confidential questions recalled from real sittings or personal attempt records. Practice results must not be presented as predicted STAT scores.
