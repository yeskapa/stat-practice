## Known source problems

- GAT 2017 and 2020: previously obtained public copies omit stimuli needed for 15 questions each. Do not import those copies as complete tests.
- GAT 2005–2009: previously inspected copies also omitted MCQ stimuli. Reconsider only with a demonstrably complete lawful source.
- GAT 2003: the previously encountered download was a composite containing 2002 pages and lacked authentic Q1–22. Reject that copy.

These findings concern the copies previously inspected, not every copy that might exist. The [current VCAA index](https://www.vcaa.vic.edu.au/assessment/general-achievement-test/past-gats) lists the newer split-format GAT papers; do not assume those match pre-2022 structure.

## Import notes and limits

Read only the relevant [conversion notes](skills/import-mock/references/conversion.md). The GAT 120-minute allowances are app adaptations for MCQs; the source booklet includes writing and a different overall schedule. Native durations belong in provenance. See [Validation](VALIDATION.md) for the scope of import checks.

Catalogue assembled from prior source manifests; live availability can change. Catalogue links were checked on 25 October 2026. Most historical downloads responded successfully; the Internet Archive fallback was rate-limited, and the SAT paper and BMAT 2019 mirror key timed out. The current GAT 2021 publisher download above works; its old /Documents URL returned 404. Availability checks do not establish completeness or rights. If unavailable, report the missing paper/key or use a matching publisher archive rather than guessing.
