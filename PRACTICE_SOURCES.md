# Further practice

For specific papers and import notes, start with the [source catalogue](MOCK_SOURCES.md).

- [ACER STAT preparation](https://stat.acer.org/au/preparation): official STAT preparation information.
- [VCAA past GATs](https://www.vcaa.vic.edu.au/assessment/general-achievement-test/past-gats): official question books and answers. Newer split-format papers differ from the pre-2022 papers in the catalogue.
- [UAT-UK preparation archive](https://esat-tmua.ac.uk/tara-preparation-materials/): TSA and BMAT Section 1 question papers and keys, alongside TARA preparation resources.

These provide additional reasoning practice in different formats. They are not calibrated substitutes for STAT. Import only the intended reasoning section and preserve its source identity and timing. See the [content policy](CONTENT_POLICY.md) before sharing converted material.
